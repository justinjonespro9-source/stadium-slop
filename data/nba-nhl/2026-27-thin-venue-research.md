## Shared buildings

TD Garden, United Center, Crypto.com Arena, and Xfinity Mobile Arena (Stadium Slop slug `wells-fargo-center`) each have one building menu. None of the 2026–27 sources described a separate NBA menu and NHL menu.

- **Xfinity Mobile Arena.** Aramark. Sixers and Flyers share the stand map. The building rename is not reflected in the Stadium Slop slug.
